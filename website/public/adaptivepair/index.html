What adoption would require

Three proof points before integration.

A useful proposal for real survey customers should withstand difficult data, preserve interpretability and avoid placing an engineering burden on a small team.

Get the gaps right

Compare win-rate scores and bias-adjusted preference estimates; report calibration and uncertainty, not just ordering.

Test against messy behavior

Stress-test disagreement, skip behavior, dropouts, respondent segments and non-transitive preferences.

Make adoption optional

Specify a minimal next-pair API, deterministic fallback, fairness checks and offline operation before proposing integration.
