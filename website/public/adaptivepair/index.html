Research note: This is a simplified browser demonstration, not a production confidence calculation.
Each click in this demo is modeled as a new synthetic respondent. The intervals resample respondents; they do not yet adjust for adaptive assignment propensities, representativeness, or survey nonresponse. Do not use these numbers for real customer decisions.
Built around practical decisions

Two engines. One clear workflow.

Designed to complement an existing survey product, not replace its respondent interface or its familiar reporting.

01 / SELECT

Choose the next pair

A balanced, coverage-aware selector proposes an eligible matchup. Experimental adaptive policies are available through the backend.

02 / MEASURE

Track the gaps

Retain familiar observed win rates while separately checking model-based comparisons and uncertainty.

03 / QUALIFY

Report what is supported

Use warnings and interval estimates to distinguish visible score differences from differences the available data can actually justify.

Research and integration

Built for evaluation, not hype.

The Python/FastAPI backend combines matchup selection and respondent-aware bootstrap diagnostics in one API. The standalone browser experience above is illustrative and does not call that service. Adaptive-sampling calibration and customer-data validation remain open work.
